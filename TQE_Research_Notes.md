## Theory of The Question of Existence (TQE) — Foundational Framework (detailed)
Premise
- I assume the origin of the universe does not require a creator or any external agency. A "null-state" suffices in which energy can be present, and energy carries an intrinsic informational property: directionality ($I$) toward complexity.
- In the pre-law phase there is no classical space–time ontology; the irreducible Heisenberg uncertainty ($\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \gtrsim \hbar/2$; $\Delta E \cdot \Delta t \gtrsim \hbar/2$) is a structural trait of the "nothing." Minimal uncertainty is therefore unavoidable.

Initiation — large fluctuations enabled by uncertainty
- Irreducible uncertainty allows large energy excursions on short timescales. Some fluctuations can cross a critical threshold, beyond which the system does not relax back but tips into a stabilizing trajectory.
- From that point on, energy is not passive: an informational directionality ($I$) emerges as an intrinsic property that orients the system toward complexity.

Emergence and "learning" of I
- $I$ is not an external control signal; it emerges from fluctuations as an internal orientation. Operationally $I$ can be modeled by a state-dependent update rule that is self-referential (remembers its own past) and signal-sensitive (responds to fluctuations and their correlations):
  
$$
I_{t+1} = \gamma \cdot I_t + \alpha \cdot |\Delta E_t| + \beta \cdot \text{corr}(\Delta E_t, \Delta E_{t-1})
$$
  
- Intuitively, larger, pattern-bearing excursions "teach" directionality; $I$ increases when the system shifts toward states that better sustain order and complex structures.

Modulation and selection in superposition
- The probability over states ($\psi$) is modulated jointly by energy ($E$) and informational directionality ($I$):

$$
P'(\psi) = P(\psi) \cdot f(E, I)
$$
  
- This modulation implements selection in the space of superpositions: states whose $(E, I)$ combination supports complexity gain weight, steering the system toward law-compatible subsets.

Threshold and law lock-in
- As $E$ and $I$ jointly enter a critical window (e.g., $X = E \cdot I$ reaches the proper range), the system reaches a "ready" configuration: the superposition strongly prefers certain law-sets.
- Law lock-in follows: a quasi-stationary, self-sustaining set of laws stabilizes (conserved quantities, symmetries, governing interactions). Operationally, key variables exhibit persistently small relative changes.

Collapse and classical phase
- Once the "learned" energy configuration has selected the law-set, the system effectively collapses onto the privileged branch and classical expansion begins.
- During expansion, laws become final (lock-in), and subsequent dynamics proceed under these stabilized laws. Heisenberg uncertainty still holds, but stabilized laws channel fluctuations, preventing the kind of large excursions that could seed a new universe within the existing one.

Consequence
- In my view, a universe can arise "from nothing" through an internal mechanism: energy endowed with intrinsic directionality ($I$), enabled by uncertainty-driven large fluctuations, selects through superpositional modulation, stabilizes laws (lock-in), and then expands classically.

## Framework (concise)
The TQE is a theoretical framework; in my view, stable physical laws emerge from the primitive coupling of Energy ($E$) and Information ($I$). Laws and (effective) constants are not axiomatic; during cosmogenesis they stabilize through a probabilistic selection mechanism.

Key claims
- Physical laws are not pre-given; they emerge from pre-law quantum fluctuations.
- Stability arises from the coupling of vacuum energy fluctuations with an information-theoretic orientation parameter ($I$).
- This coupling yields a "Goldilocks window" where law-governed universes can stabilize.
- The framework yields falsifiable predictions (e.g., statistical patterns in CMB anomalies).

### Core equation

$$
P'(\psi) = P(\psi) \cdot f(E, I)
$$

Interpretation
- $P(\psi)$ — "space of possibilities": probability distribution over $\psi$-states (law-sets, quantum states, even neural configurations). It encodes the ensemble of possibilities before reality "chooses" a concrete state.
- $E$ — Energy: the system's physical energy content (e.g., vacuum energy, chemical/electrical energy).
- $I$ — Information / internal structure: a measure of order, coherence, or structural complexity; in TQE $I$ is an intrinsic property of energy that carries directionality.

## 2025-11-16 — Note (Hawking singularity theorems and the TQE pre-law phase)
Claim (classical theorems)
- The Hawking–Penrose singularity theorems show that if GR’s conditions hold — energy conditions, focusing of time-/null-like geodesic congruences, global hypersurface, smooth metric, valid classical gravity — then past-directed geodesics have finite length → a mathematical singularity follows.

Remark
- These theorems apply strictly within a classical, well-defined space–time. The singularity is a necessity of GR’s mathematical structure, not a physical "point."

TQE viewpoint
- In the pre-law phase — as defined in TQE — there is no metric, no classical space–time, no Einstein tensor, no geodesic structure. The system is governed by a probabilistic field and the modulation $P'(\psi) = P(\psi) \cdot f(E,I)$. Therefore the theorems' prerequisites do not hold.

Conclusion
- Without classical space–time, the singularity concept itself is not defined. The "initial singularity" is not a physical entity but the limit of GR’s extrapolation. In TQE, classical space–time — and thus classical gravity — emerges only after law lock-in from the pre-law informational dynamics.

Intuitive summary
- No space–time → no gravity. No gravity → no singularity. The beginning is not a collapsed point but a pre-physical, energy–information selection process from which coherent space–time emerges.

## Heisenberg uncertainty in the pre-law phase
Premises
- In the pre-law phase there is no classical space–time ontology (no metric, no geodesics).  
- The "nothing" is not perfect emptiness; it is a boundary state with irreducible uncertainty.
- The uncertainty relations ($\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \geq \hbar/2$; $\Delta E \cdot \Delta t \geq \hbar/2$) are better read as a structural trait of the null-state, not merely a "measurement limit."

TQE reading
- Uncertainty guarantees a lower bound on fluctuations; perfect rest is impossible.  
- Large energy excursions ($\Delta E$) can therefore occur and push the system beyond the lawless regime — an initiating mechanism for cosmogenesis.
- Informational directionality $I$ can manifest during fluctuations (self-organizing orientation), and via $P'(\psi) = P(\psi) \cdot f(E,I)$ it preferentially weights structure-compatible outcomes.

Formal sketch
- Heuristic start: $\Delta E \cdot \Delta t \gtrsim \hbar/2$ → a nontrivial energy window exists on short timescales.  
- Modulation update: $P'(\psi) = P(\psi) \cdot f(E, I)$, normalized by $Z$; $f$ increases law-compatible mass, $I$ encodes orientation.  
- Large excursions are not deterministic; they are necessary but not sufficient. Actual lock-in depends on $I$ and on the $E$–$I$ balance (e.g., $X = E \cdot I$; $|E - I|$).

Consequences after lock-in
- Uncertainty persists, but stabilized laws channel fluctuations, preventing macro-scale universe-seeding excursions within an existing universe.
